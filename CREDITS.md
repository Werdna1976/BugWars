## Sound effects

From Kenney's sound packs (www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero
(CC0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Trimmed, pitched and
level-matched for the game; files in `public/sfx`.

- Impact Sounds: deploy, bites, heavy hits, pot and Nest hits, squish, tower fall (layered)
- Interface Sounds: seed shot, honey shot, card click, countdown tick, "Go"
- Sci-Fi Sounds: spit, Bombardier spray, area blast, tower fall (layered)
- Digital Audio: victory and defeat stingers

`nestWake.ogg` (the swarm buzz when a Nest wakes) is synthesized for this game.
